### 💫 About Me

I am a **multidisciplinary engineer** and **faceless creator** focused on ideas over identity. My work spans the full stack of digital creation, from hardware registers to high-level game mechanics.

* **🛠️ Systems Engineering:** Specializing in **Rust**, **C**, and **Linux** environments to build performant, privacy-focused tools.
* **⚛️ Applied Physics:** Integrating scientific reasoning into software design.
* **🎮 Interactive Media:** Developing anime-style games and simulations using **Godot**.
* **🛡️ Philosophy:** Advocating for open-source, privacy, and digital minimalism.
